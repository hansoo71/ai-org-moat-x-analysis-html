Core argument: In AI, product surfaces, categories, interfaces, and early technical advantages are becoming easier to copy. The durable moat shifts toward the organization itself: talent density, status hierarchy, authority distribution, customer proximity, mission, and the institution that lets a particular kind of person become exceptional. Founders should ask what kind of person can only become themselves here; employees should distinguish emotional validation from structural authority, economics, and scope.
